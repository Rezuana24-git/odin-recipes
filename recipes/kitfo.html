Kitfo dish

Kitfo is hand chopped high quality raw meat Ethiopian traditional food.

Main ingredients

  • Red meat
  • butter
  • chili
  • garlic

    • Steps

    1. Melt the butter in a saucepan over low
    2. Add garlic, ginger and other spicy
    3. Add the chopped red meat
    4. mix them very well

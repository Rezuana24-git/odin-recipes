Kitfo

Kitfo dish

Kitfo is hand-chopped high-quality raw meat, a traditional Ethiopian dish.

Main ingredients

  • High-quality red meat (lean)
  • Niter kibbeh (spiced clarified butter) or regular butter
  • Mitmita (chili spice blend)
  • Garlic
  • Ginger (optional but common)

Steps

  1. Melt the butter in a saucepan over low heat.
  2. Add minced garlic, ginger, and mitmita (or other spices).
  3. Add the finely chopped red meat.
  4. Mix thoroughly until the meat is evenly coated and just warmed (do not fully cook if serving the traditional raw version).
